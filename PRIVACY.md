# Privacy Policy — Open Amp

**Effective date:** September 11, 2026

## The short version

Open Amp processes your guitar **entirely on your device**. It collects,
transmits, and shares **no personal data**. No accounts, no analytics, no
telemetry, no ads.

## Permissions and how they're used

- **Microphone / audio input** — the live guitar signal from your USB audio
  interface (or built-in mic) flows through the amp simulator in real time.
  Audio is processed in memory and **never recorded, stored, or sent
  anywhere**. The looper feature holds audio in on-device memory only, for
  the length of your session.
- **USB device access** — to enumerate and use your USB audio interface.
  No USB data leaves the device.
- **Local storage** — presets you save are written to the app's private
  on-device storage. They never sync anywhere (we have no servers).

## Third parties

None. No third-party SDKs, no trackers, no advertising.
